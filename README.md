## 🌟 Executive Summary & Concept

**PANCH VIGHNA** (*Five Obstacles*) is a purpose-built, high-fidelity browser game submission crafted for the **NIAT Ganesh Chaturthi Game Design Contest**.

Unlike traditional mini-game compilations or reskinned arcade titles, **PANCH VIGHNA** is designed as **ONE continuous narrative festival journey** spanning the five quintessential celebrations of Ganesh Chaturthi.

### 🎯 The Core Game Design Rule
**Every stage exercises a distinct cognitive and physical gameplay skill:**
1. **Chapter 1: RANGOLI** ➔ *Visual Memory + Continuous Gesture Tracing*
2. **Chapter 2: PANDAL** ➔ *Spatial Circuit Reasoning + Generator Capacity Planning*
3. **Chapter 3: MODAK** ➔ *Culinary Sequencing + Oscillating Steamer Timing Precision*
4. **Chapter 4: DHOL** ➔ *Call-and-Response Rhythm Reproduction + Beat Accuracy*
5. **Chapter 5: VISARJAN** ➔ *Procession Route Strategy + Mid-Journey Vighna Adaptation*

---

## 🪔 Cultural Reverence & Philosophy

The design strictly upholds authentic Hindu cultural reverence:
1. **Sacred Dignity**: Lord Ganesha is never harmed, attacked, damaged, cracked, or treated as a health bar.
2. **Systemic Challenges**: All obstacles (*Vighnas*) and failure states belong strictly to environmental, logistical, and craft challenges (electrical generator power limits, steam pressure timing windows, rhythmic call reproduction, procession crowd management).
3. **Overcoming Obstacles**: As *Vighnaharta* (the Remover of Obstacles), Ganesha inspires the community to overcome every challenge with focus, patience, joy, and devotion.

## 📜 The Five Chapters (Vighnas)

### 🌸 Chapter 1: RANGOLI — Pattern & Sacred Geometry
- **Primary Skill**: Visual Memory + Tracing
- **The Challenge**: A morning gust of wind threatens the sacred courtyard pattern before the prayers begin.
- **Gameplay Mechanics**:
  1. **Memorization Phase (2.2s)**: The sacred geometric Kolam illuminates sequentially through numbered sacred nodes.
  2. **Tracing Phase**: The player draws a continuous stroke via cursor or finger touch through the nodes in the exact sequence.
  3. **Payoff**: Completing the pattern unleashes a vibrant blossom of pink and yellow gulal powder across the stone floor.
- **Scoring**: Node sequence accuracy + stroke completion percentage + speed bonus (0–100 PTS).

---

### 🏛️ Chapter 2: PANDAL — Spatial Circuit & Generator Grid
- **Primary Skill**: Spatial Circuit Reasoning + Capacity Constraint Management
- **The Challenge**: The pandal generator is capped at **75W** under the *Limited Power Vighna*.
- **Gameplay Mechanics**:
  1. **Circuit Grid**: Interactive schematic wiring between the Main Power Generator and pandal appliances (Toran Entrance Lights [25W], Altar Brass Diyas [15W], Ceiling Canopy [20W], Sound System [25W], Dholak Mic [15W]).
  2. **Resource Constraint**: Essential loads (Entrance Lights + Altar Diyas = 40W) are mandatory. The player must complete the power circuit without exceeding 75W.
  3. **Payoff**: Powering on the grid triggers a warm, golden illumination cascade across the grand pandal.
- **Scoring**: Essential connection fulfillment + non-overload safety margin + aesthetic efficiency (0–100 PTS).

---

### 🥟 Chapter 3: MODAK — Culinary Kitchen & Steaming Precision
- **Primary Skill**: Multi-Step Sequencing + Steamer Timing Precision
- **The Challenge**: Devotees await fresh prasad modaks before the grand aarti begins.
- **Gameplay Mechanics**:
  1. **Dough Press**: Press the freshly kneaded rice flour dough onto the wooden patla.
  2. **Filling Pairing**: Select the matching sacred filling (*Coconut Jaggery*, *Kesar Saffron*, or *Dry Fruit Pista*).
  3. **Apex Pleat**: Pinch the apex into 21 traditional sacred folds.
  4. **Steamer Precision Gauge**: An oscillating steam pressure indicator cycles across the dial. The player must lift the steamer lid precisely in the **Golden Zone (60–85%)**—avoiding raw (<60%) or oversteamed (>85%) offerings.
  5. **Payoff**: Plate hot, aromatic modaks onto the sacred banana leaf tray.
- **Scoring**: Recipe accuracy + golden-zone steaming accuracy + consecutive perfection streak (0–100 PTS).

---

### 🥁 Chapter 4: DHOL — Call-and-Response Rhythm Engine
- **Primary Skill**: Auditory & Visual Pattern Memory + Rhythm Reproduction
- **The Challenge**: The procession drummers sound out festive talam calls that the player must echo with precision.
- **Gameplay Mechanics**:
  1. **The Call**: The Master Dholak plays a syncopated rhythmic motif (Dha, Tak, Dhum). Expanding visual pulse rings radiate from the instrument head to guarantee **100% sound-off accessibility**.
  2. **The Response**: The player reproduces the pattern on the Dhol and Tasha with matching timing and beat values.
  3. **Payoff**: Harmonious drum rolls, rising crowd cheers, and golden resonance waves.
- **Scoring**: Hit accuracy delta (|t_player - t_expected| in ms) across 5 progressive rounds (0–100 PTS).

---

### 🌊 Chapter 5: VISARJAN — Route Strategy & Eco-Friendly Immersion
- **Primary Skill**: Strategic Route Planning + Dynamic Adaptation
- **The Challenge**: Guide the grand Chariot procession to the Sacred Ghat through crowded city streets.
- **Gameplay Mechanics**:
  1. **Route Selection**: Choose between 3 paths with distinct length, crowd density, and blessing multipliers (*Main Bazaar Avenue*, *Lake Promenade*, *Temple Garden Lanes*).
  2. **Dynamic Vighna Event**: Sudden obstacles strike mid-route (*Sudden Monsoon Showers* or *Aarti Crowd Surge*).
  3. **Real-time Adaptation**: Decide whether to take a sheltered bypass detour or hold steady with tarpaulins.
  4. **Eco-Friendly Clay Murti (शाडूची माती)**: The authentic clay murti gently merges with the sacred water, releasing floating brass diyas, marigold petals, and celebratory chants (*"गणपती बाप्पा मोरया, पुढच्या वर्षी लवकर या!"*).
- **Scoring**: Route clearance efficiency + dynamic obstacle response bonus (0–100 PTS).
